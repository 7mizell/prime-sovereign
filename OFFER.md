# PRIME SOVEREIGN LISTINGS — Service Definition

## Core Offer: "Listing Launch Kit"
**Price:** $500–$1,500 per listing (tiered by property value)
**Retainer:** $2,000–$4,000/mo for 4–8 listings/mo (teams/brokerages)

---

## DELIVERABLES PER LISTING

### Tier 1 — Essential ($500) | Properties <$500k
- **Property Description** (250 words, MLS-optimized, SEO-rich)
- **5 Social Media Captions** (IG/FB/LI + hashtags)
- **Email Announcement** (to agent's sphere database)
- **Feature/Benefit Bullet Sheet** (for showings/brochures)
- **Turnaround:** 24 hours

### Tier 2 — Professional ($1,000) | Properties $500k–$1.5M
- Everything in Tier 1
- **Neighborhood Guide** (500 words: schools, dining, walkability, commute)
- **Video Script** (60-sec walkthrough narration for Reels/TikTok)
- **Open House Flyer Copy** (headline, body, CTA)
- **Buyer Objection Handler** (top 5 objections + responses)
- **Turnaround:** 24 hours

### Tier 3 — Sovereign ($1,500) | Luxury $1.5M+
- Everything in Tier 2
- **Lifestyle Narrative** (1,000-word emotional story for premium brochure)
- **Drone Shot List** (specific shots for videographer)
- **Virtual Staging Brief** (room-by-room style direction)
- **Press Release Draft** (for notable sales/records)
- **Seller Net Sheet Copy** (clear, confident language)
- **Priority Turnaround:** 12 hours
